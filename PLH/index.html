Our Merch Range

Our Play Like Her merchandise is designed to promote confidence, comfort and participation in women’s sport. Each item represents the strength and determination of female athletes, and by wearing our merch, you are supporting the movement for greater opportunities, recognition and respect in sport.

Every purchase makes a difference. 100% of profits support our partner organisations: Women’s Sport Australia, Play Like a Girl and the Australian Sports Commission.
